# Sealed

Search the world's unread letters, logbooks and ciphers from one search box.

Sealed searches eight archives at once, live from your browser:

| Archive | What it searches |
|---|---|
| Prize Papers, War of American Independence | Every word of 885 transcribed pages from 11 captured ships (bundled in the app) |
| Brieven als Buit (Letters as Loot) | Every word of 2,419 Dutch letters seized at sea |
| Library of Congress | Full text of transcribed diaries, letters and manuscripts |
| VOC General Missives | Every word of 569 Dutch East India Company reports |
| Europeana 1914–1918 | WWI family letters, diaries and postcards |
| New Bedford Whaling Museum | 2,038 whaling logbooks and journals (Internet Archive) |
| Internet Archive manuscripts | Diaries and correspondence before 1930 |
| DECODE | 10,000+ historical ciphers, keys and manuals |
